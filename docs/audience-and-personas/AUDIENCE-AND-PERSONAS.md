## 1. Executive Market Summary & Positioning

APEST Teams occupies a unique space at the intersection of **ecclesial theology**, **organizational diagnostic**, and **continuous leadership team formation**.

### The Core Problem: The $10 Pricing Anchor Trap
* The individual 5Q Vocational Assessment sells for **$10**.
* Secular organizational tools (like Patrick Lencioni's *Working Genius*) sell for **$25** and bundle a team map for free.
* If APEST Teams were priced as a per-person markup (e.g., $100/person/year), church buyers immediately divide by $10 and perceive a 10x penalty for software reporting.
* **The Strategic Resolution:** **Price the team and the organization, not the seat.**
  * Base Tier: **$499/year** (up to 5 seats, plus $79/additional seat) or **$1,250/year** for an organizational team license.
  * Why this works: Church boards and executive pastors do not compare $1,250 to a $10 test; they compare it to a **$5,000 church consulting engagement, a $3,000 staff retreat, or an executive coaching retainer**.

## 2. Target Audiences

### A. Primary Segment: Church Leadership & Staff Teams (70% of Market)
* **Organization Profile:** Established churches (150 – 2,000+ attendance) or multi-site campuses.
* **Team Size:** 3 to 12 members (Pastors, Directors, Ministry Department Heads).
* **Pain Point:** Plateaued growth, pastoral burnout, "Sunday production hamster wheel," structural suppression of apostolic pioneering and evangelistic outreach, inward-facing Shepherd-Teacher institutional equilibrium.
* **Decision Maker:** Senior / Lead Pastor and Executive Pastor.

### B. Secondary Segment: Church Plant Core Teams & Launch Networks (20% of Market)
* **Organization Profile:** Pre-launch or early-stage church plants (0–3 years).
* **Team Size:** 3 to 8 founding leaders (Planting couple, core team leaders, volunteer apprentices).
* **Pain Point:** Misalignment between visionary planter (usually A or P) and operational executors; danger of premature institutionalization; needing to balance fivefold DNA before planting public gatherings.
* **Decision Maker:** Lead Planter, Network Planting Director, or Denominational Assessor.

### C. Tertiary Segment: Denominational & Network Executives (10% of Market / High LTV)
* **Organization Profile:** Church planting networks, dioceses, presbyteries, district offices overseeing 20–200 churches.
* **Team Size:** Executive cabinet overseeing multi-team cohorts.
* **Pain Point:** Inability to diagnose why certain plants thrive and others fail; lack of portfolio visibility across regional leadership pipelines; desire to offer scalable team coaching without deploying human consultants to every site.
* **Decision Maker:** Network Director, Bishop, or Regional Superintendent.

### Persona 1: Marcus Webb — "The Isolated Architect"
* **Title:** Lead Pastor (Plant Founder, 9 years tenure)
* **APEST Profile:** **A-T (Apostle 46, Teacher 35, Prophet 27, Shepherd 13, Evangelist 9)**
* **Formal Authority:** Yes (Final decision rights)
* **Psychographic & Emotional Reality:**
  * Feels alone carrying the entrepreneurial risk and future trajectory of the church.
  * Casts big vision, but observes staff meetings quickly devolving into calendar logistics and pastoral maintenance.
  * Frustrated that his team nods in agreement but fails to execute apostolic initiatives.
  * Blind spot: Tends to treat people as functional chess pieces for missional objectives, under-valuing relational pacing.
* **What Marcus Needs from the Platform:**
  * Objective validation that his apostolic voice is structurally isolated in the team.
  * Awareness of how his formal authority unintentionally silences the prophetic and evangelistic voices.
  * Concrete partnering rituals with James (Shepherd) so his vision doesn't burn out staff.

---

### Persona 2: James Okafor — "The Operational Stabilizer"
* **Title:** Executive Pastor (4 years tenure)
* **APEST Profile:** **S-T (Shepherd 48, Teacher 37, Prophet 21, Evangelist 12, Apostle 7)**
* **Formal Authority:** Yes (Controls budget, personnel, operations)
* **Psychographic & Emotional Reality:**
  * Protects the staff from Marcus's weekly brainstorms and sudden strategic pivots.
  * Constantly managing staff morale, emotional health, and operational friction.
  * Experiences apostolic tension as "unnecessary organizational chaos and threat to staff burnout."
  * Blind spot: Naturally pulls the organization into equilibrium and safety. Equilibrium in living systems theory is stagnation.
* **What James Needs from the Platform:**
  * Recognition that his high Shepherding score is an invaluable asset, but when paired with veto authority, it acts as an institutional dampener on mission.
  * A clear framework for constructive conflict (APEST Thinking Hats) rather than peace-faking.
  * Confidence that the 12-week formation plan will increase health without overloading staff capacity.

---

### Persona 3: Priya Nair — "The Prophetic Reformer"
* **Title:** Director of Community Formation (3 years tenure)
* **APEST Profile:** **P-S (Prophet 47, Shepherd 36, Evangelist 24, Teacher 16, Apostle 8)**
* **Formal Authority:** No
* **Psychographic & Emotional Reality:**
  * Deeply attuned to covenant integrity, justice, spiritual hypocrisy, and genuine heart formation.
  * Senses when church programs are spiritually hollow even when attendance numbers look healthy.
  * Often hesitates to speak during meetings because her critique sounds negative or disruptive to the Shepherd-Teacher consensus.
  * Feels isolated when hard questions are brushed off in favor of efficiency.
* **What Priya Needs from the Platform:**
  * Structural permission and airtime for the prophetic voice without being branded "cynical" or "unsupportive."
  * Pairings with Sofia (Prophet <-> Evangelist) to unite internal holiness with outward gospel welcome.
  * Structured pre-flight rituals that invite her discernment before major decisions are voted on.

### Persona 4: Sofia Reyes — "The Marginalized Herald"
* **Title:** Outreach & Neighboring Director (2 years tenure)
* **APEST Profile:** **E-S (Evangelist 45, Shepherd 38, Prophet 20, Teacher 14, Apostle 8)**
* **Formal Authority:** No
* **Psychographic & Emotional Reality:**
  * Deep passion for unchurched people, the margins of East Denver, and inviting strangers into community.
  * Feels intellectually intimidated by Marcus and James's theological discourse and dense curriculum focus.
  * Frustrated that outreach is treated as a minor department line item rather than the heartbeat of church purpose.
* **What Sofia Needs from the Platform:**
  * The platform's diagnostic to expose that she is the **only primary Evangelist** on the entire leadership team.
  * Clear data showing that the church's 3-year attendance plateau directly correlates with the suppression of her voice.
  * Actionable equipping tools to train the congregation in outward hospitality.

### Persona 5: Daniel Park — "The Misunderstood Worshipper"
* **Title:** Worship & Arts Pastor (2 years tenure)
* **APEST Profile:** **P-T (Prophet 46, Teacher 38, Shepherd 22, Evangelist 11, Apostle 6)**
* **Formal Authority:** No
* **Psychographic & Emotional Reality:**
  * Carries immense prophetic and doctrinal depth (P 46, T 38).
  * Has been pigeonholed into "song leader and sound equipment technician" because Western ecclesiology lacks a framework for a prophetic artist.
  * Feels restless preparing musical setlists when his deepest desire is to awaken the church to the transcendence and holiness of God.
* **What Daniel Needs from the Platform:**
  * Liberating realization that his role title does not constrain his fivefold calling.
  * Recognition by Marcus and James that Daniel is a primary theological and prophetic asset.
  * Guidance on using musical and liturgical liturgy to disciple the congregation in prophetic holiness.

## 4. The 3 Purchasing Moments

Unlike consumer SaaS, church leadership teams buy in three distinct episodic moments:

| Moment | Trigger | Mindset | Value Delivered |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. The Configuration Review** | Annual staff retreat or strategic planning window | "We need to understand who we are and why we feel stuck." | Full team baseline, 5-layer diagnostic, 12-week formation plan. |
| **2. The Change Review** | Staff turnover, key departure, new hire, or reorganization | "We just lost our Executive Pastor / Hired a Youth Director. How does this change our team balance?" | Delta simulation, re-calculation of coverage tiers, risk analysis. |
| **3. The Annual Reading** | Annual budget review or leadership retreat | "Did our health improve after 12 months of formation?" | Longitudinal score tracking, maturity progression, covenant renewal. |
