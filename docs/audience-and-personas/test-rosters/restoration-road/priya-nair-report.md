## 3. Primary Capacity Deep Dive: Prophetic (Score: 47)

### Overview
Prophetic leaders maintain the spiritual integrity and alignment of God’s people. They carry an intuitive antenna for spiritual drift, moral compromise, and religious hypocrisy, calling the church back to covenant intimacy and social righteousness.

### Core Characteristics
1. Deeply attuned to God’s heart, grieving what grieves Him.
2. Unflinching instinct to name the "elephant in the room."
3. Distinguishes quickly between cultural religiosity and true discipleship.
4. Champion of the poor, marginalized, and overlooked.
5. Experiences frustration when meetings bypass spiritual reality for administrative minutiae.
6. Operates with moral passion and spiritual discernment.
7. Prepared to stand alone in defense of divine truth.

### Key Functions
* **Expose Spiritual Compromise:** Questions programs that produce consumers rather than saints.
* **Stand for Justice:** Ensures the church's practices reflect God's righteousness in the neighborhood.
* **Cultivate Contemplative Discernment:** Slows the community down to listen to the Spirit before acting.
* **Call for Repentance:** Names corporate sins of apathy, comfort, and omission.
* **Guard Integrity:** Protects the gap between what the church confesses and how it lives.

### Blind Spots & Shadows
* Prophet's grief curdling into cynicism, bitterness, or spiritual superiority.
* Impatience with gradual discipleship processes; demanding immediate sanctification.
* Withholding fellowship when institutions fail to meet the prophetic ideal.
* Dropping emotional or relational grenades at the end of meetings out of pent-up frustration.
