## 3. Primary Capacity Deep Dive: Prophetic (Score: 46)

### Overview
The prophetic gift carries an urgent concern for God's presence, purity, and covenant alignment. Prophetic reformers strip away superficial religiosity to reveal what is eternal and true.

### Core Characteristics
1. Possesses a deep, contemplative prayer life and sensitivity to God's holiness.
2. Experiences pain when corporate worship or programs feel staged or spiritually shallow.
3. Penetrating diagnostic precision; cuts through corporate rhetoric in moments.
4. Drawn toward ancient practices, new monasticism, and radical discipleship.
5. Patient in silence; waits until the essential truth must be named.
6. Refuses to treat church leadership as mere management.

### Key Functions
* **Disrupt Institutional Complacency:** Shatters comfortable illusions with quiet, unshakeable conviction.
* **Guard Worship Integrity:** Ensures corporate worship leads to encounter rather than entertainment.
* **Theological Discernment:** Interrogates whether current practices can actually sustain biblical discipleship.
* **Call to Sacred Rhythm:** Reclaims Sabbath, confession, and contemplative stillness.

### Blind Spots & Shadows
* Withdrawing into brooding cynicism or spiritual elitism when the church does not change fast enough.
* Dropping devastating observations that leave the team immobilized by guilt.
* Contempt for necessary operational logistics and administrative details.
