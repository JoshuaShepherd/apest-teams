## 3. Primary Capacity Deep Dive: Apostolic (Score: 46)

### Overview
Apostolic individuals are wired to initiate new expressions of Jesus’ kingdom, pioneer into uncharted spaces, and protect the foundational DNA of the church. In mature expression, apostles mobilize leaders, build systems for multiplication, and steward organizational resilience across transitions.

### Core Characteristics
1. Visionary and future-oriented; lives mentally two to five years ahead.
2. Catalyzed by starting new things, uncomfortable with administrative maintenance.
3. Crosses boundaries effortlessly: intellectual, cultural, and geographic.
4. Systems-minded; diagnoses root structural causes rather than treating symptoms.
5. High tolerance for ambiguity and calculated risk in pursuit of the mission.
6. Thrives on momentum; resists institutional status quo and equilibrium.
7. Builds translocal networks and scalable frameworks.

### Key Functions
* **Extend Mission Frontiers:** Pushes the community beyond internal comfort toward underserved populations.
* **Steward Movement DNA:** Preserves irreducible theological and operational values.
* **Align Systems with Mission:** Restructures governance, staffing, and budgets to serve outward multiplication.
* **Mobilize Leaders:** Equips others to take responsibility and plant new works.
* **Navigate Chaos:** Functions with clarity and strategic boldness amidst volatility.

### Blind Spots & Shadows
* Vision calcifying into non-negotiable dogma.
* Moving at a pace that causes relational debris and team burnout.
* Viewing team members instrumentally as vehicles for vision execution.
* Impatience with pastoral care and slow developmental processes.
* Frustration with institutional maintenance and routine operational rhythms.

### Impact & Posture
* **Extension — Living as One Who Is Sent:** Let vision provoke collaborative imagination rather than compliance. Balance apostolic drive with the complementary insights of prophets, shepherds, and evangelists.

## 4. Secondary Capacity Deep Dive: Teaching (Score: 35)

### Overview
The teaching capacity brings clarity, order, and comprehension. Rather than merely conveying data, the teacher constructs pathways of understanding that form disciples into Christlikeness.

### Core Characteristics & Functions
* Explores systemic theology, biblical texts, and missiology with rigorous discipline.
* Creates curriculums, models, diagrams, and learning environments.
* Translates deep concepts into accessible language.
* Patient with intellectual development; eager to see people think faithfully.

### Blind Spots & Shadows
* Treating intellectual comprehension as the equivalent of spiritual obedience.
* Overwhelming team members with theoretical nuance when action is required.
* Impatience with emotional or intuitive decision-making.
