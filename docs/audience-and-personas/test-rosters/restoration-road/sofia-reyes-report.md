## 3. Primary Capacity Deep Dive: Evangelistic (Score: 45)

### Overview
Evangelists are the storytellers and bridge-builders of the Kingdom. They carry a passionate heart for those outside the faith, making the good news tangible, accessible, and compelling. They ignite the community’s imagination for the lost.

### Core Characteristics
1. Instinctively turns outward; scans the room for the stranger and newcomer.
2. Infectious joy and natural charisma in non-religious environments.
3. Translates faith into warm, ordinary, non-churchy language.
4. Celebrates the first signs of spiritual curiosity in seekers.
5. Sees people as treasures to be welcomed rather than projects to be fixed.
6. Intuitive cultural translator; easily bridges the church with the street.

### Key Functions
* **Build Bridges to the Culture:** Creates non-threatening entry points for the unchurched.
* **Cultivate Invitation Culture:** Models contagious hospitality for the whole church.
* **Humanize the Gospel:** Keeps the focus on Jesus’ grace, warmth, and mercy.
* **Challenge Inward Drift:** Reminds the congregation that it exists for those not yet here.

### Blind Spots & Shadows
* Avoiding difficult moral challenges or hard theological truths to preserve rapport.
* Mistaking enthusiasm and attendance growth for mature discipleship.
* Resenting the intellectual depth of the Teacher or the moral challenge of the Prophet.
