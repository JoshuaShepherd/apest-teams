## 3. Primary Capacity Deep Dive: Shepherding (Score: 48)

### Overview
Shepherds are called to protect, nurture, and guide the community into holistic spiritual and relational health. They specialize in hospitality, pastoral presence, reconciliation, and deep interpersonal belonging.

### Core Characteristics
1. Instinctively tracks the emotional temperature and relational health of the team.
2. Inexhaustible capacity for empathetic listening and compassionate care.
3. Natural mediator; bridges conflicts and seeks communal peace.
4. Fiercely protective of vulnerable members and newcomers.
5. High loyalty to individuals; values long-term commitment.
6. Reluctance to endorse changes that risk alienating or wounding people.
7. Finds deep joy in seeing wounded people healed and integrated into community.

### Key Functions
* **Cultivate Belonging:** Designs communal spaces where people feel fully seen and safe.
* **Pastoral Protection:** Shields the community from predators, divisiveness, and spiritual harm.
* **Reconcile Fractures:** Restores broken relationships through patient mediation.
* **Sustain Long-Term Formation:** Walks alongside people through prolonged crises.
* **Operational Stability:** Ensures reliable, predictable support systems for daily life.

### Blind Spots & Shadows
* Prioritizing communal peace and pleasant feelings over necessary repentance.
* Coddling immaturity; enabling people to stay comfortable rather than challenged.
* Acting as an institutional shock-absorber that suppresses apostolic momentum.
* Subconsciously defending institutional equilibrium because change feels dangerous.
