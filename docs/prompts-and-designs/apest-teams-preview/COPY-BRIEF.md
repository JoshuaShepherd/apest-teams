## What APEST Teams is

APEST is the fivefold pattern from Ephesians 4 — apostolic, prophetic, evangelistic, shepherding, teaching — understood in Alan's work as **functions given to a whole body, not offices held by a few people**.

The 5Q Assessment (at 5Q Central, unaffiliated, connected to Alan's 5Q work) reads **one person's** vocational pattern. APEST Teams reads **a group's**. Each participant attaches the 5Q report they already hold; nobody fills in a new questionnaire and nobody uploads a colleague's PDF. The output is one document that every participant reads — no manager's edition, no private scoring, no ranking of people.

The report has six movements, and material is labeled by how it was derived so a reader can tell arithmetic from argument:

| Movement | Label | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| The ledger | Transcribed | Each person's five scores as they appear in their own report, with dates |
| Coverage | Computed | Which functions are held strongly, thinly, or by nobody |
| Concentration | Computed | Whether this is a real ecology or four versions of one instinct |
| Where authority sits | Interpreted | Which function decision-making defers to; which it can ignore unnoticed |
| What this pattern produces | Interpreted | The predictable fruit and the predictable blind spot |
| One thing to try | Assignment | A single thirty-day practice at the thinnest function, plus a debrief question |

Team size is 3–12 people. **Pricing is not decided — do not write any.**

## Positioning and tone

- The hook is diagnostic and slightly uncomfortable, not aspirational. The current placeholder specimen finding is: *"Four of your five leaders lead by tending. Nobody here is charged with going."* That's the register — a sentence that came out of five assessments and a page of arithmetic, not a personality workshop.
- It must not read as a team-building exercise, an org-health survey, or a strengths tool.
- Three refusals are load-bearing and should stay explicit: it is **not** a hiring or performance instrument (no one is ranked or found wanting; using it to justify a personnel decision is misuse); **not** a personality workshop (functions Christ gives a body, not temperament types); **not** Alan speaking (it reasons from his published work and cites it, but does not impersonate him or replace community and discernment).
- Because it isn't open, the page's credibility comes from candor: say plainly it's in development, say what a team should have ready, don't over-promise a date.

## Constraints

- **No pricing, no price ranges, no "starting at."**
- **Only two outbound destinations** may be linked: the APEST theme page (`/themes/apest`) and the 5Q Assessment (`https://5qcentral.com/tests/`). Do not reference APEST Local, AI Lab, courses, or cohorts.
- Sentence-level: plain words, active voice, no em-dash-heavy stacking, no "unlock/empower/journey/game-changer," no emoji, no exclamation points. British-inflected plainness is fine; Alan's own voice is direct and slightly prophetic, never chirpy.
- American or Australian spelling — pick one and be consistent with the rest of the site.
