### P7 — how it works

Eyebrow: `How it works`
Headline: `Three steps. One shared document.`
Intro: `The process is designed so that no single participant has more information than another. One person opens the team and invites the group. Each participant attaches their own existing 5Q report. Everyone reads the same output.`

### P8 — three steps

**01 · `Open and invite`**
`One person opens a team and invites between three and twelve participants. No other setup is required at this stage. Participants receive a link; they do not need to coordinate with each other before joining.`

**02 · `Attach your own report`**
`Each participant attaches the 5Q report they already hold from 5Q Central. Nobody fills in a new assessment here, and nobody submits or sees another person's report. Each person brings only what is already theirs.`

**03 · `Read together`**
`When all reports are in, every participant receives the identical document. There is no manager's edition, no private scoring, no ranking. The report is written to be read by the whole group at once.`

### P9 — report section

Eyebrow: `The report`
Headline: `Six movements, each labeled by how it was derived.`
Intro: `The report does not mix arithmetic with argument without telling you which is which. Each section carries a label — transcribed, computed, interpreted, or assigned — so you can see exactly what kind of claim you are reading.`

### P10 — six movements

Each card: a derivation label, a title, a body.

**`Transcribed` · `The ledger`**
`Each person's five scores as they appear in their own 5Q report, with dates, transcribed without alteration. This is the raw material everything else is built from.`

**`Computed` · `Coverage`**
`A computed reading of which functions are held strongly across the group, which are held thinly, and which nobody in the room carries at any meaningful level.`

**`Computed` · `Concentration`**
`A computed reading of whether this group represents a real ecology of functions or four variations of the same instinct. Concentration is different from coverage — and often more diagnostic.`

**`Interpreted` · `Where authority sits`**
`An interpreted reading of which function decision-making in this group tends to defer to — and which function can be consistently ignored without anyone noticing.`

**`Interpreted` · `What this pattern produces`**
`An interpreted reading of the predictable fruit this configuration tends to generate, and the predictable blind spot it tends to carry. Both are named plainly.`

**`Assigned` · `One thing to try`**
`A single thirty-day practice aimed at the group's thinnest function, with a debrief question. One assignment, not a program. The report does not prescribe beyond this.`

### P12 — what it is not (dark band, right column)

Eyebrow: `What it is not`

**`Not a hiring or performance instrument`**
`No participant is ranked, scored against another, or found wanting. The report reads a group's pattern, not the adequacy of its members. Using it to justify a personnel decision is a misuse of what it is designed to do.`

**`Not a personality workshop`**
`APEST describes functions Christ gave to his Body, not temperament types or psychological categories. The report reasons from Ephesians 4 and published work in that tradition. It is a theological diagnostic, not a self-understanding exercise.`

**`Not Alan Hirsch speaking`**
`The report reasons from published work and cites it, but it does not impersonate Alan or substitute for the community discernment and pastoral judgment that any reading of this kind requires.`
