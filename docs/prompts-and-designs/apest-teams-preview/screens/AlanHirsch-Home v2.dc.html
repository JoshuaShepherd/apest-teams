Themes

Six doorways into Alan's work

Alan's work spans more than four decades. These six themes are not content categories so much as different ways into one larger question: what does it take to recover and sustain apostolic movement?

Explore all six themes →
mDNA
The risen Christ coded six elements into his church – an irreducible pattern that, when recovered, releases the kind of movement we read about in Acts. Discover the missional DNA already within you.
Metanoia
Repentance goes deeper than behavior change – it is the renovation of imagination, the reorientation of an entire life toward the reign of Christ. This pathway walks you into that transformation.
Reframation
Recover the Hebraic worldview behind the New Testament and rediscover the gospel as Jesus announced it – holistic, embodied, and alive with kingdom meaning.
Discipleship
Every movement in history began with disciples following Jesus and making more disciples. Rediscover discipleship as the simple, reproducible, and lived center of everything the church is meant to be.
Movement Intelligence
The kingdom of God moves through networks, neighborhoods, and systems – often underground, always unstoppable. Discover the patterns of how Christ's movement grows and what our role is within it.
APEST
Apostolic, prophetic, evangelistic, shepherding, and teaching intelligence – five functions given to the whole body, not a few offices. Learn to recognize them, name them, and put them to work together.

About Alan Hirsch

About Alan Hirsch

Alan Hirsch is known for helping the church recover its apostolic nature – and rediscover movement as its native expression. His work centers on a Christological conviction: the risen Christ already coded into his church everything it needs for movement. Our task is recovery, not invention. From leading a missional community in Melbourne's inner city to shaping networks across six continents, Alan's journey reflects the theology he teaches – lived practice inseparable from deep reflection. His current work focuses on translating four decades of frameworks, writing, and teaching into formational resources that equip leaders wherever they are.
